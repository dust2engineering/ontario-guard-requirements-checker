# Worked example 1 — backyard deck, 1,500 mm drop, one house (Part 9)

Drop 1,500 mm > 600 mm → guard required. Exterior, serving one dwelling unit,
walking surface 1,500 mm above ground (≤ 1,800 mm) → minimum height **900 mm**.
Had the deck been 2,000 mm above ground, the minimum would be 1,070 mm.
Openings: 100 mm sphere; nothing climbable between 140 and 900 mm.

Command: `python3 guards.py part9 ext_low 1500`
