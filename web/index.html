The rules in brief

Part 9 (9.8.8.1): a guard is required on every unprotected side where the drop is more than 600 mm (adjacent surface considered within 1.2 m of the edge). Heights run from 900 mm (inside a dwelling unit; low exterior decks serving one unit) to 1,070 mm generally and 1,500 mm for exterior stairs over 10 m up. Openings must block a 100 mm sphere (150 mm for the triangular stair opening, 200 mm in industrial occupancies), and between 140 and 900 mm up, nothing may facilitate climbing.
