# Worked example 2 — mezzanine edge in a Part 3 commercial unit

Drop over 600 mm → guard required at the general Part 3 minimum of
**1,070 mm** (Article 3.3.1.18). Loads per 4.1.5.14: 0.75 kN/m or 1.0 kN
concentrated horizontally, 1.5 kN/m vertical.

Command: `python3 guards.py part3 general 2400`
